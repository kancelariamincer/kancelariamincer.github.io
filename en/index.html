PRACTICE AREAS

  • Mediation and settlements – civil law

    The law office provides comprehensive legal services in the broad field of civil law, including legal advice and the preparation of legal analyses and opinions. Civil law is the most extensive branch of Polish law, covering matters ranging from debt recovery to inheritance and infringements of real property ownership. We offer not only professional legal assistance but, above all, representation before courts and public authorities.

    Scope of assistance

    We can assist you with:

    • drafting and negotiating contracts, and preparing various letters and documents
    • representation at the pre-litigation stage, conducting negotiations and concluding settlements
    • representation in mediation
    • debt recovery and enforcement proceedings
    • law of obligations cases – in particular those arising from non-performance or improper performance of contracts, torts (all kinds of damages claims) and actio pauliana claims
    • property law cases – in particular those concerning ownership of real property, dissolution of co-ownership and infringement of possession
    • company law cases, including the liability of management and supervisory board members
    • unfair competition cases
    • protection of personal interests

  • Inheritance law

    We handle inheritance matters with full professionalism and commitment. We provide legal assistance as early as the stage of drafting a will. After the opening of the succession, we conduct proceedings from the declaration of acquisition of the inheritance through to the division of the estate, and we pursue claims for the legitime (zachowek) on behalf of our clients.

    Statements of claim and applications

    We will help you prepare court pleadings:

    • Application for a declaration of acquisition of an inheritance
    • Statement of claim by a creditor of the deceased against an heir for payment
    • Application for division of an estate
    • Statement of claim for the legitime (zachowek)
    • Statement of claim for a declaration that an heir is unworthy to inherit
    • Statement of claim for exclusion of a spouse from inheritance
    • Statement of claim for a declaration that a will is invalid
    • Statement of claim for performance of a legacy
    • Statement of claim to compel performance of a testamentary instruction
    • Application to amend a decision declaring the acquisition of an inheritance
    • Application to supplement a declaration of acquisition of an inheritance

  • Personal injury

    We offer professional legal assistance in obtaining compensation. We help clients obtain the compensation they are entitled to from insurance companies when payment has been refused. Unjustified refusals and significantly understated amounts are common practices among insurers, and we challenge them. We support clients from the moment the damage occurs, through the claims-handling process, to court proceedings and payment of the final amount.

    Types of cases

    Among others, we seek compensation for damage caused by:

    • road traffic accidents
    • accidents at work
    • farm accidents
    • medical malpractice
    • improper care of a sick or disabled person
    • falls on uneven or snow-covered pavements

  • Business legal services

    The law office provides ongoing legal services to businesses, including partnerships and companies governed by the Commercial Companies Code. We offer comprehensive legal advice on all aspects of our clients’ business activity.

    Ongoing legal services

    Ongoing legal services for businesses include, among others:

    • providing information on changes in the law
    • drafting and reviewing commercial contracts
    • preparing legal opinions and guidance on the application of the law
    • preparing minutes of meetings of company bodies
    • preparing and filing applications for company registration and for changes in the court register
    • handling the dissolution and liquidation of companies, as well as their transformations and divisions
    • preparing for and attending meetings of company bodies
    • keeping registers of contracts and legal documents
    • representation before administrative authorities and administrative courts, as well as common courts
    • drafting employment contracts and notices of termination, and participating in talks with trade unions
    • ongoing receivables management – from payment demands, through court representation, to supervision of bailiff enforcement

  • Criminal law

    We represent clients at all stages of criminal proceedings, fiscal penal proceedings and petty offence proceedings.

    We act as

    • defence counsel for the accused in petty offence proceedings
    • counsel for the injured party, the auxiliary prosecutor, the private prosecutor and non-party persons
    • defence counsel for persons detained or held in pre-trial detention
    • defence counsel for convicted persons in enforcement proceedings

    • Statement of claim by a spouse for permission to use the family home
    • Statement of claim for annulment of marriage
    • Application by a foreign national for permission to marry by proxy
    • Application for consent to the marriage of a minor
    • Application to entrust the exercise of parental authority to one parent
    • Application to limit parental authority
    • Application to deprive, suspend or restore parental authority
    • Application to establish contact with a child
    • Application to prohibit contact with a child
    • Application for permission to perform an act exceeding the ordinary management of a child’s property
    • Statement of claim to establish/deny maternity
    • Statement of claim to establish/deny paternity
    • Statement of claim for a declaration that the acknowledgement of a child is ineffective
    • Application for adoption
    • Statement of claim to dissolve an adoption
    • Application to establish legal guardianship and appoint a legal guardian for a minor
    • Application to establish guardianship

  • Property law

    The law office has experience in handling cases in the broad field of property law. We provide legal advice and represent clients in court proceedings concerning ownership of movables and real property. We also handle cases concerning limited property rights, including perpetual usufruct, usufruct, easements, pledges, mortgages and the cooperative ownership right to premises.

    Statements of claim and applications

    Among others, we prepare pleadings initiating court proceedings:

    • Application for dissolution of co-ownership
    • Application for a declaration of acquisition of ownership by adverse possession
    • Application to establish an easement of necessary access and other easements
    • Statement of claim to abolish a land easement against payment
    • Statement of claim to establish ownership
    • Owner’s statement of claim for recovery of property (rei vindicatio)
    • Owner’s statement of claim to cease interference (actio negatoria)
    • Statement of claim against a property owner to cease nuisance (immissions)
    • Statement of claim for demarcation of real property
    • Statement of claim for purchase of an occupied portion of land
    • Statement of claim to compel transfer of ownership of real property
    • Statement of claim to bring the land and mortgage register into line with the actual legal status
    • Statement of claim to establish acquisition of a thing from an unauthorised person
    • Application to appoint an administrator of a thing
    • Statement of claim for remuneration for use of a thing, return of benefits and compensation for wear and tear
    • Statement of claim by an autonomous possessor for reimbursement of outlays
    • Statement of claim by an autonomous possessor for transfer of ownership of land occupied by a building, against appropriate payment
    • Statement of claim against a perpetual usufructuary for damage caused by improper use of the land
    • Statement of claim by a usufructuary against the owner for reimbursement of outlays
